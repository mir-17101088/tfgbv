# Story concept (draft for Mir's approval — no code yet)

Client: Tech Global Institute. Design system: `Desktop\Tech Global Institute Design System` (Deep Teal #174D4F primary; Montserrat / Roboto / Lato; no emoji; AI imagery never depicts real or realistic people).

## The one idea
**"She did everything right."** Every time the system told her what to do, she did it — and every step cost her time, dignity and peace. The reader should finish exhausted, the way she is, and angry on her behalf.

Working title options (pick/rewrite): *Block Him* · *Five Years for a Message* · *The Complainant* · *Everything She Was Told to Do*

## Hard numbers from her own timeline (computed, not researched)
| Beat | Law says | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| First message → case filed | Cognizable offence: police must record FIR (CrPC s.154 — verify) | **45 days**; GD first, then 4 offices in 24 hours (thana → DMP → DB → thana) |
| Case filed → investigation done | **30 working days**, max **75** with approvals (Pornography Control Act 2012 s.5) | **211 days / ~151 working days** to charge sheet |
| Charge sheet → anything | — | **3 years 8 months of silence (1,346 days)** |
| Then | She is the complainant | **Two non-bailable warrants issued against her**; "You are a woman. How would it look…" |
| First time she sees the accused | — | **1,830 days** after the first message. No cross-examination. Next date 7 Dec 2026 |

Other counts: 8 messages to PCSW (6 just asking for an update); screenshots sent twice; 5 witnesses asked of her (4 demanded, then 2–3 more); signed the ejahar twice; 3 hours to draft it; 50 min waiting for the ADC; 1 wasted court trip during Eid + fuel crisis.

## Structure (scroll story, ~8 chapters)
1. **Hero** — a phone screen lighting up in the dark at 10:41 PM. Headline, dek, byline only. Notification count ticks up as you arrive.
2. **The messages** — her real screenshots, heavily redacted (blurred explicit media, offensive text greyed, celebrity profile photo blurred). Line: "I don't respond." repeated, each time smaller.
3. **"What the law says should happen"** — a clean teal track: complaint → FIR → 30-day investigation → charge sheet → trial. Calm, short, fast. *This is the promise.*
4. **"What happened"** — the same track redrawn as a tangled, looping line that keeps doubling back. The signature visual (below).
5. **The runaround** — illustrated map scene: four buildings, one woman, 24 hours. Speech bubbles with the exact words she was told ("Go to DMP." "Go to DB." "Go home. We'll call you.").
6. **The quotes that wound** — illustrated full-bleed moments, one per scroll: "Just block him." / "Do you know Bangladesh Police doesn't hold the account's information?" / "You can't just say whatever you like because you're upset." / "You are a woman. How would it look…"
7. **The silence** — 1,346 days rendered as an endless scroll of empty day-cells (compressed with a "you've scrolled 100 days… 1,246 to go" counter, skip button for accessibility). The reader *feels* the wait.
8. **The warrant** — the paper arrives at her parents' door. Illustration of her father holding it. Then the court in Eid holidays: empty corridor.
9. **25 Oct 2026** — she finally sees his face; still no answer. "Next date: 7 December." Ticking counter of days since the first message, live.
10. **Context + what should change** — sourced stats (PCSW volume, ActionAid 85% stay silent, conviction odds), a short "if this happens to you" guide (GD vs FIR, escalate to SP/magistrate, preserve evidence), TGI sign-off.

## Signature visual (pick one — see approaches)
**"The Promise vs The Path"**: one SVG line drawn as you scroll. The law's line is a straight 75-day ruler. Her line follows it, then loops, stalls, doubles back, goes dormant for years, then crawls on — scaled to real days, so the reader scrolls ~25× longer through her line than the law's.

## Illustration direction
- Hand-drawn feel, ink line + flat teal/mint fills, one alarm colour (warrant red, used maybe 3 times in the whole piece).
- **She is never drawn with a face** (back view, silhouette, hands, phone glow) — protects identity and avoids TGI's "no realistic people" rule; officials drawn as faceless uniforms/desks/hands holding phones — it's the system, not individuals.
- Recurring motifs: the phone glow, the waiting chair, stacked paper, the clock.
- Production options: (a) SVG illustrations drawn in code (fully on-brand, animatable, light); (b) commissioned illustrator from briefs I write; (c) AI-generated stylised scenes — only abstract/non-realistic, and only with your approval per TGI rules.

## Personas (optional chapter)
Three composite "she could be" personas built only from cited data (e.g. ActionAid's 85% who never report) — clearly labelled as illustrative composites, not real people.
